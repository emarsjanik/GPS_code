INTERPRETATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The all-data plot is diagnostic only.
The primary scientific comparison is the GOOD TRACK plot.

Good-track selection is based on complete repeat tracks and does not
use the +0.242 m datum correction.
Therefore the +0.242 m comparison is an independent datum diagnostic.

If multiple independent GOOD tracks show substantially lower RMS after
+0.242 m, that is evidence that the GNSS-R solution has a common vertical
datum offset relative to the tide model.
If only one track improves, the apparent improvement may instead be track-
specific rather than a common datum correction.
